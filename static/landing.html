Features

Everything your group needs

Built for gamers, not sysadmins.

🔒

WireGuard VPN

Join a private encrypted tunnel with one click. Your PC appears on the same local network as the game server — no configuration needed.

🎮

Auto Game Detection

GamezNET scans your Steam library and non-Steam launchers automatically. Your current game is broadcast to everyone online — no manual updates.

💬

Network Chat

Floating chat panel with tabbed channels and direct messages. Snaps to the edge of the window with an unread badge. No Discord required.

🖥️

Remote Assistance

Request help from any online player directly. A peer-to-peer RustDesk session opens automatically over the VPN — no relay servers, no setup.

🎙️

Discord Integration

Full embedded Discord — browse channels, read history, send messages with your player name and Steam avatar. No Discord account required.

⚡

Steam Profiles

Link your Steam account once to show your avatar, level, game library, and top-played games on your player card visible to everyone online.

📅

Session Scheduler

Schedule game nights with a countdown visible to all players. Everyone gets a system tray notification when a session is posted.

📁

Shared File Drop

Upload mods, saves, or screenshots up to 500 MB. Set files as public or share privately with specific players. Accessible while connected.

📱

Mobile Companion

See who's online, check server status, and chat from your phone — no install required. Works on any browser with your existing invite token.

Setup

Up and running in 60 seconds

No Python, Git, or technical knowledge required.

1

Get an invite

Request access below or get a token from the network admin. Tokens are unique and tied to your VPN identity.

2

Run one command

Press Win+R, open PowerShell, paste the install command. The installer handles everything silently.

3

Activate your token

Enter the invite token in the app. Your WireGuard credentials are saved permanently — you'll never need the token again.

4

Click Connect & play

One click to join the private network. Launch your game. When you're done, click Disconnect or right-click the tray icon.

Request Access

GamezNET is invite-only. Leave your name and email and the admin will reach out.

No spam. Your info is only seen by the network admin.
